Reference-system passbands (wavelength in A, photon-counting throughput) for synthetic photometry
(tools/abscal_validation/agn_lines_synphot.py).
sdss_[ugriz].txt  SVO Filter Profile Service, SLOAN/SDSS.<b> "full transmission" (fetched 18 Nov 2026);
                  identical to STScI CDBS comp/nonhst/sdss_<b>_005_syn (SDSS DR1 response curves).
landolt_[BV].txt  STScI CDBS comp/nonhst/landolt_<b>_004_syn (Maiz Apellaniz 2006).
ps1_y.txt         SVO Filter Profile Service, PAN-STARRS/PS1.y (Tonry et al. 2012; fetched 19 Nov 2026): the
                  passband of the GSPC PS1 y magnitudes that anchor LCO Y. Shown on the website; not used by the
                  AGN correction study (FLOYDS does not reach it).
